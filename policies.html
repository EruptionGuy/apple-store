1. Order and Payment Policies

  • Order Placement: Customers can place orders directly on the Apple Store website. Ensure all information provided is accurate to avoid processing delays.
  • Payment Methods: We accept major credit cards, Apple Pay, PayPal, and other region-specific payment methods. Payment is processed upon order confirmation.
  • Pricing: Prices displayed are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. Prices are subject to change without prior notice.

2. Shipping and Delivery

  • Shipping Options: Standard and express shipping options are available at checkout. Shipping fees vary by region.
  • Delivery Times: Estimated delivery times will be provided during checkout. Delays may occur due to unforeseen circumstances (e.g., weather or customs clearance).
  • Tracking: Customers will receive a tracking link via email upon shipment.

3. Returns and Refunds

  • Return Policy: Items may be returned within 14 days of receipt, provided they are in their original condition and packaging.
  • Non-Returnable Items: Personalized or custom-configured products and opened software cannot be returned.
  • Refund Process: Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after receipt of the returned product.

4. Warranty and Repairs

  • Apple Limited Warranty: Most Apple products come with a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
  • AppleCare+: Extended warranty and accidental damage coverage are available with AppleCare+ plans.
  • Repairs: For repairs under warranty, please contact Apple Support or visit an authorized service provider.

For inquiries, reach out to Apple Support. Assistance is available 24/7 in most regions.
